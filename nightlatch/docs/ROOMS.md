# NIGHT LATCH — Room Specifications (art + interaction contract)

Every room is generated by `art/rooms/<id>.py` (see `art/rooms/hotel_room.py` as the
reference implementation) and exported to `public/assets/rooms/<id>/` as `bg.png`,
`bg_<variant>.png`, `obj_<name>[_<variant>].png` and `room.json`.

**Contract with the game code** (`src/content/rooms/*.ts`): the hotspot ids, state-object
names, point ids and variant names below are referenced by the scripts. Keep them exact.

Conventions
* Viewport height is **144** px. Width 320 unless stated (wider rooms scroll).
* A standing adult is ~50 px tall at scale 1.0 (feet at y≈140). Doors ≈ 1.15× a person.
* `sc.scale(yFar, sFar, yNear, sNear)` must be set; keep sFar ≥ 0.70.
* Walkable polygon(s) in screen space; every hotspot's `walk` point must be inside them.
* `face` is one of up/down/left/right (the direction Rook faces when using the hotspot).
* Exits are ordinary hotspots (ids starting `exit_`); their `walk` point should be near the
  screen edge or doorway.
* State objects (`state=True`) are drawn only when the game says so; static occluders get a
  `baseline` (screen y of their front-most floor contact).
* Points: at least `start` plus each `from_<room>` entry point listed.
* `sc.meta['floor']` = wood | stone | metal | carpet | tile | grass | water (footstep sounds).
