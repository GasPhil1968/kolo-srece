# NIGHT LATCH — Chapter Design

Each chapter: **locked-room opening → investigation → turn**. Puzzle steps are listed in
dependency order; `→` means "enables". IDs in `code` are the identifiers used in
`src/content`. The machine-checked dependency graph is generated by `npm run validate`
(see `docs/PUZZLE_GRAPH.md`).

---

## Chapter 1 — "Room Nine" (Monday, Oct 15)

**Locked room:** Room 9, Hotel Sorrento. Rook wakes on the floor, bruised, six days missing.
**Mechanism:** paper-and-wire key trick (door locked from outside, key in the far side of
the lock).

| # | Step | Requires | Gives |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Search the bed | — | `newspaper` (date: Oct 15; headline "COMMISSION STENOGRAPHER MISSING"; gala on Sat) |
| 2 | Open wardrobe, take hanger | — | `hanger` |
| 3 | Hanger + radiator (lever it straight) | `hanger` | `wire` |
| 4 | Newspaper on gap under door | `newspaper` | paper under door |
| 5 | Wire in keyhole (refuses if no paper: "it'd land on the hall carpet") | `wire`, paper under door | key on paper |
| 6 | Pull the newspaper back | key on paper | `key9`, `newspaper` back |
| 7 | Key on door | `key9` | exit to lobby |
| opt | Take hat from bedpost | — | clue `hat_note`: *"If you're reading this, they got to you. Don't drink anything Sal pours. Trust Mabel. — T.R."* |
| opt | Pocketbook (examine) | — | clue `pocketbook`: pages from Oct 9 torn out; last entry *"Oct 8 — N.M. Believe her."*; back cover *"On ice — B3. Sorry about the smell."* |

**Lobby:** clerk Pettibone holds Rook's coat; house rule "key for coat" → give `key9` →
`lighter` (engraved *"To Tommy — from Frank. Partners. 1938."*), `matchbook` (Blue
Lantern; inside: *"Tue 11"*). Pettibone: two men brought him in — a big man in a camel-hair
coat, and a silent one who "smelled like a hospital" (clue `two_men`). Register: "Mr. Smith
& cousin… Do not disturb. Patient sleeps." (clue `register`). Map opens: Office, Blue Lantern.

**Office:** Mabel: six days gone; Nora came on Oct 8 with a folder; *"Ask the piano"* (clue
`ask_piano`); Mabel was a court stenographer for twelve years. Client **"Evelyn Marsh"**
waits: hires Rook to find her "sister" Nora; gives `apt_key`, `nora_photo`, `retainer`.
Observed: watch pinned upside down, carbolic soap (clue `evelyn_watch`); phone number
Harbourview 4-170 (clue `evelyn_phone`); claims to be Nora's sister (clue `evelyn_claim`).
Filing cabinet: thin Meridian file, case closed by Lt. F. Delaney (clue `meridian_file`).
Phone: Delaney is warm and warns him off.

**Nora's flat (Bellwether):** ransacked. Wastebasket → `carbon`; carbon held to the window
light → readable letter to Rook (clue `nora_letter`: "the Meridian fire was no accident").
Gramophone record *"Lullaby of the Lamplighter — Deacon Boone Trio"* (clue
`lamplighter_record`; plays the theme). Framed photo of Walter and Nora (clue
`family_photo`). Flour tin footprint with heel plate (flavor).

**Deduction (tutorial):** `ask_piano` + `lamplighter_record` → `piano_is_deacon`.

**Blue Lantern:** Sal offers a free drink — if the hat note was read Rook tips it into the
potted palm (the palm wilts for the rest of the game); otherwise he declines. Deacon only
talks business after `piano_is_deacon` ("Play 'Lullaby of the Lamplighter'"): Rook brought
Nora here on the 8th; Deacon keeps her safe; *"You made me promise… 'If I come back asking,
it means they've got a leash on me'"*; *"You said the proof was on ice. Pier 9."* → Pier 9.

**Turn:** night at Pier 9; the cold store door ajar; a shadow; a sap. *End of Chapter One.*

## Chapter 2 — "On Ice" (Tuesday, Oct 16)

**Locked room:** walk-in freezer, Pier 9 cold store. **Mechanism:** thermal — rock salt
melts the brine ice holding the release handle; the lighter thaws the spindle.

| # | Step | Requires | Gives |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Take ice pick from the workbench | — | `ice_pick` |
| 2 | Hanging cod, row B, third fish (labels on the beam) | `ice_pick` (and clue `pocketbook` for Rook to know which) | `minutes1` (transcript p.1, attendees; the lieutenant's name has run) |
| 3 | Rock salt sack | — | `rock_salt` |
| 4 | Salt on brine tub (handle frozen inside) | `rock_salt` | ice softened |
| 5 | Ice pick on softened tub | `ice_pick`, softened | `release_handle` |
| 6 | Handle on spindle | `release_handle` | handle fitted ("frozen solid") |
| 7 | Lighter on spindle | `lighter`, handle fitted | spindle thawed |
| 8 | Turn the handle | thawed | door opens |
| req | Halcyon crates, pry with ice pick | `ice_pick` | `vial` (L-7), clue `shipping_label` (Gullwing → Halcyon via Calypso) |

**Pier 9 (day):** Gus Tolliver: saw Rook with Walter's girl on the 8th; last night two men
dragged Rook into the cold store (camel coat; white jacket under a raincoat). The Meridian:
hammering at the hatch ~half an hour before the fire; a big detective "with a nice coat"
called him drunk (clue `gus_hammering`).

**Kessler's Pharmacy:** give `vial` → "come back in an hour" (any travel). Result: refined
scopolamine + barbiturate; compliance and amnesia; masked with anise (clues
`lethe_identified`, `anise`); Halcyon used to buy scopolamine from him (clue
`halcyon_scopolamine`).

**Gazette:** Abernathy on Wendell Pike (missing since August, investigating Halcyon House,
clue `pike_missing`); clippings: Meridian "no foul play — Lt. Frank Delaney" (clue
`clipping_meridian`), Rook suspended for "erratic conduct" Nov 1949 (clue
`clipping_suspension`). Pike's press card on the corkboard (`press_card`; No. 417). Pike's
drawer, 3-wheel combination **4-1-7** ("Pike used his press number for everything") →
`pike_camera` (film still inside) and clue `pike_notes` (three Meridian dockhands admitted to
Halcyon Nov '49, referred by "a police lieutenant", paid by "G.H.").

**St. Brigid's Mission:** Sister Agnes on "the forgotten" who come down from Gull Hill
(clue `mission_forgotten`); Mick Kerrigan's nightmare testimony — a big man padlocking the
hatch, "Go home, Mick" (clue `kerrigan`); a headscarfed volunteer who won't meet Rook's eyes
(clue `volunteer`, pays off in Ch6).

**Deduction:** `lethe_identified` + `pocketbook` (six days torn out) → `i_was_drugged`.

**Turn:** evening at the Gazette; "Evelyn" arrives with a thermos. Anise. Rook remembers
Kessler's words, excuses himself, takes Pike's camera into the darkroom. *End of Chapter Two.*

## Chapter 3 — "Safelight" (Wednesday, Oct 17)

**Locked room:** the Gazette darkroom, padlocked from the **inside** with Rook's own
padlock. Grease-pencil note on the enlarger: *"YOU DID THIS. THE KEY IS SAFE. DEVELOP THE
FILM. NO WHITE LIGHT TILL THE PRINTS ARE FIXED. — T."* **Mechanism:** photographic process;
the past self photographed the key's hiding place.

| # | Step | Requires | Gives |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Camera into the changing bag (film can't see even red light) | `pike_camera` | film in tank |
| 2 | Developer → tank | film in tank | |
| 3 | Stop bath → tank (wrong order refused with explanation) | developer done | |
| 4 | Fixer → tank | stop done | |
| 5 | Wash tank at the sink | fixer done | `negatives` (wet) |
| 6 | Hang negatives on the drying line, take them dry | `negatives` | dry negatives |
| 7 | Negatives in the enlarger | dry negatives | |
| 8 | Paper box → expose & tray-process a contact sheet | negatives in enlarger | `contact_sheet` (frames: Gullwing truck at Halcyon's back gate; Vey shaking hands with a big man holding a gold lighter; the darkroom's wall timer with its back open and a key inside) |
| 9 | White-light switch (refused until prints fixed) | `contact_sheet` | white light: the dark corner is revealed — the wall timer and red grease-pencil writing invisible under the safelight: *"ANISE = LETHE. THE 'SISTER' BROUGHT THE COFFEE. THERE IS NO SISTER."* (clue `no_sister`) |
| 10 | Stool under the timer, slide the back panel | white light | `padlock_key` |
| 11 | Key on padlock | `padlock_key` | exit |

**Investigation:**
* **Cemetery:** Ruth & Walter Marsh's stone: "…mother of their only child, Nora" (clue
  `only_child`). Fresh chrysanthemums, card *"Papa — I'm being brave. Soon. N."* dated
  Sunday Oct 14 (clue `flowers`). Gravedigger Cobb: the girl comes at dawn on Sundays, in a
  headscarf (clue `scarf`).
* **Harbor Commission:** Lillian: Oct 3 night session (clue `oct3_session`) — the
  Commissioner, a thin doctor with a cane, and a big police lieutenant in a camel coat (clue
  `meeting_attendees`); Nora's steno pad went to Miss Pruett's out-tray. Persuade Lillian
  (show `contact_sheet` or tell her Nora is in danger) to phone Pruett upstairs → take
  `steno_pad` from the tray. Crane's diary on Pruett's desk: gala broadcast Sat 9 PM (clue
  `gala`); Gullwing letterhead (clue `crane_gullwing`).
* **Office:** Mabel transcribes `steno_pad` (Pitman): "…the Calypso… the 21st… witnesses
  treated… 'Rook is your department. You handled him once.'" (clue `steno_transcript`).
* **1st Precinct:** Delaney (camel coat on the rack, clue `delaney_coat`; gold gull lighter,
  clue `gull_lighter`; "I got you into a rest home in '49", clue `rest_home`). Officer Pruitt
  lets Rook use the reverse directory: Harbourview 4-170 = Halcyon House (clue
  `phone_halcyon`).
* **Hask's:** Rook's lock picks are collateral for the padlock he bought on the 16th; pay
  with `retainer` → `lockpicks`.
* **Blue Lantern:** Sal, confronted with what Kessler found, breaks: a cop holds his
  brother's markers; "he wears a nice coat" (clue `sal_confession`).

**Deductions (required):** `evelyn_claim` + `only_child` (or `family_photo`/`no_sister`) →
`evelyn_impostor`; `phone_halcyon` + `evelyn_watch` → `evelyn_nurse`.
Optional: `two_men` + `meeting_attendees` + `delaney_coat` → `camel_coat`.

**Turn:** Halcyon House unlocks. Dusk, rain; Rook climbs the wall; a torch beam; "Good
evening, Mr. Rook." — Vivian in a nurse's cape. A needle. *End of Chapter Three.*

## Chapter 4 — "Room Eight" (Thursday, Oct 18)

**Locked room:** padded isolation Room 8, Halcyon House. Loudspeaker: *"Good morning,
Mr. Rook. Welcome back… And when you find her — do call."* Scratched near the cot leg:
*"T.R. 11/49"* (clue `been_here`). **Mechanism:** smoke, not fire — trigger the fire
procedure that unlocks every room.

| # | Step | Requires | Gives |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Look through the observation window | — | clue `fire_procedure` ("FIRE: ring bell, unlock ALL rooms, patients to the lawn") |
| 2 | Press call button → orderly's tray | — | `spoon`, `cup` (water), `pills` (palmed) |
| 3 | Spoon on the bare concrete strip | `spoon` | `flat_spoon` |
| 4 | Flat spoon on the vent grate (stand on cot) | `flat_spoon` | grate open: `matches` + clue `pike_note` ("Smoke, not fire.") |
| 5 | Flat spoon on mattress | `flat_spoon` | `straw` |
| 6 | Straw + cup | `straw`, `cup` | `damp_straw` (dry straw is refused: "smoke, not a cremation") |
| 7 | Damp straw in vent | `damp_straw`, grate open | |
| 8 | Matches on vent | `matches` | smoke → fire bell → doors unlocked |

**Halcyon corridor:** patients herded to the lawn. Pike (Patient 14) wanders the corridor;
`press_card` → fragments: trucks at the back gate, "the man in the camel coat with the gull
lighter" (clue `pike_recalls`). Nurses' station roster. Records office door: `lockpicks`.

**Vey's office / records:** desk drawer → small cabinet key; cabinet "1949":
`halcyon_files` — ROOK, Thomas, Room 8, 2–9 Nov 1949, referred by Det. Lt. F. Delaney,
"L-series… Meridian inquiry memories suppressed. Recommend reinforcement" (clue
`rook_file`); Doyle/Kerrigan/Fisk and Pike, all referred by Delaney (clue `witness_files`).
Appointment book: "Sat 20 — Gala 9 PM. Calypso loads Sat 23:00, sails 02:00" (clue
`calypso_schedule`). Clocks everywhere.

**Escape:** laundry cart → laundry van → bottom of Gull Hill (cutscene).

**Turn:** Office. Rook tells Mabel. A knock: Delaney and two uniforms — "Thomas Rook,
you're under arrest for the abduction of Nora Marsh. Her sister swore out a complaint."
"Her sister doesn't exist, Frank." A truncheon. *End of Chapter Four.*

## Chapter 5 — "Holding" (Friday, Oct 19)

**Locked room:** holding cell, 1st Precinct. Everything Rook carried is confiscated.
Cellmate Barnaby (ex-Calypso deckhand). Night desk: Officer Danny Pruitt.
**Mechanism:** persuasion by deduction — convince the jailer with a contradiction and a
reconstructed timeline; then fish the key ring with a blanket.

| # | Step | Requires | Gives |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Talk to Barnaby | — | clue `calypso_rocks` ("machine parts" that are rocks; Gullwing ship) |
| 2 | Talk to Pruitt: the Vandermeer case (rapport) | — | rapport |
| 3 | Ask Pruitt for the Meridian evidence box (Barnaby chimes in) | rapport | `padlock` (locked shut, hasp still through it), `gate_log`, clues `report_open_hatch`, `report_time`, `gus_statement`, `shift_roster`, `fire_log`, `gate_walter`, `gate_delaney` |
| 4 | Present `padlock` against "hatch found open" | evidence box | contradiction proven (`hatch_locked`) |
| 5 | Timeline (Notebook): order 6 cards — 22:05 Walter signs in "for Sgt. Rook"; 22:25 Delaney's car; 22:40 hammering; 23:00 shift in the hold; 23:15 fire reported; 23:30 Delaney "arrives" | the six clues | deduction `delaney_lied`; Rook reads *"for Sgt. Rook"* — he sent Walter aboard |
| 6 | Present the timeline to Pruitt | `delaney_lied`, `hatch_locked` | Pruitt "goes for coffee", hangs the keys nearer the bars |
| 7 | Take blanket from bunk | — | `blanket` |
| 8 | Blanket on key ring | `blanket`, keys moved | `cell_keys` → cell open |

**After the cell:** Delaney's office: desk drawer (`lockpicks` are confiscated — use the
cell keys' small desk key) → belongings envelope (all confiscated items return) and
Delaney's pocketbook: "Sat — Calypso loads 23:00. Tail Boone — he'll lead us to the girl"
(clue `delaney_plan`). Evidence room shelf: Gus's original statement (flavor).
**Hask's:** the padlock is one of twenty sold to Gullwing Holdings in Sept '49; the order
was signed for by F. Delaney — "he haggled" (clue `padlock_order`).

**Turn:** Blue Lantern after closing. "Delaney's going to tail you to her." Deacon: "Come
downstairs, where nobody listens." A bolt slides. "Forgive me, Tom. You made me promise."
*End of Chapter Five.*

Harbourview 4-170: "St. Brigid's Mission. She's here." Nora: "Tom? Who did you call?"
"…I don't know." Cars. Kroll. *End of Chapter Six.*

## Chapter 7 — "Below the Waterline" (Saturday, Oct 20)

**Locked room:** cargo hold of the MV Calypso, Pier 4. Coat (and every item in it) taken.
Nora is in the forward hold, reachable through a vent pipe. **Mechanism:** cooperation —
two prisoners, one pipe; stacking light crates; cutting the hatch tarpaulin.

| # | Step | Requires | Gives |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Hatband (the old hiding place) | — | `matches` (two) |
| 2 | Match on the ship's lantern on its hook | `matches` | hold lit (dark → lit background) |
| 3 | Knock on the vent pipe → talk to Nora | lit | Nora's side: ladder, her hatch bolted; she has her father's knife |
| 4 | Pry a "MACHINE PARTS" crate? Too heavy. Halcyon crates are light; one is free | lit | crate 1 placed under the cargo net |
| 5 | Push the oil drum aside | lit | crate 2 freed → stacked |
| 6 | Ask Nora to pass the knife through the pipe | talked to Nora | `walter_knife` |
| 7 | Climb the crates → grab the cargo net → push a hatch board | stack | board lifts against the tarpaulin |
| 8 | Knife on the tarpaulin | `walter_knife` | out on deck |
| opt | Knife on a "MACHINE PARTS" crate's slats | `walter_knife` | clue `rocks_seen` |

**Calypso deck:** slide the bolt on the forward hatch → Nora hides behind the lifeboat.
Kroll guards the gangway. Knife on the lashing rope of an empty barrel stack → barrels
thunder down the deck → Kroll investigates. Captain's cabin: Rook's coat (all items return);
Delaney's briefcase, combination **2-2-6** — Frank's old badge number, which Rook remembers
because old memories are intact (clue `badge_226`, remembered on looking at the briefcase) →
`calypso_manifest` (insured "machine parts"; telegram from A.C.: "CALYPSO LOST AT SEA
EXPECTED. CLAIM READY."). Down the gangway.

**Preparation (Hask's back room is the safe house):**
* Nora: Walter's knife has a hollow handle → `minutes3` (names Delaney).
* **Plan**: Crane's radio address tonight at 9 from City Hall, WBRK. Get into the booth.
* **Hask** cuts a booth key from his 1938 City Hall install book → `booth_key`.
* **Gazette**: Abernathy reads the evidence, can't print it (Crane holds the paper's loan),
  gives a gala `press_pass`.
* **Pier 9**: Gus gives his own 1949 watch log for Walter → `gus_log`.
* **Kessler**: physostigmine — "clears the fog for an hour" → `antidote`.
* (Optional) Mabel; Sal (returns Rook's hat note? flavor); Mission (Agnes).

**Turn:** City Hall, the gala. Nora goes for Captain Morrow. In the service corridor Vivian:
"Mr. Rook." A needle in the neck. He staggers into the booth and turns the key.
*End of Chapter Seven.*

## Chapter 8 — "On Air" (Saturday, Oct 20, 8:47 PM)

| 2 | Use the antidote ampoule | `antidote` | the blur clears |
| 3 | Engineer's card on the wall (patch order) | — | clue `patch_card` |
| 4 | Patch bay: BOOTH MIC → PROGRAM LINE (close-up) | `patch_card` | patched |
| 5 | Unscrew the ON AIR bulb with a handkerchief (hot) | — | `bulb`; Delaney won't see the lamp |
| 6 | Wait for nine (clock) → Crane begins | patched, bulb out | |

| 9 | Talkback to Delaney with the mic live — confession dialogue (present lighter photo, the 22:25 gate entry, "for Sgt. Rook") | | confession on air |
| 10 | Unlock the door → the clock tower roof | | |
| 11 | Roof: Delaney at the edge with a gun. Give him the `lighter` ("Partners.") | `lighter` | surrender |

**Epilogue:** Crane arrested at the podium; Vey and Vivian at Halcyon; patients freed;
Pike files the story; the Meridian case reopened. Pier 9 at dawn with Nora — Rook tells her
about the gate log if he hasn't. "He went because he wanted to. That was my father. And you
went back for him two years later with half your mind gone. That's you." Final narration,
credits.
