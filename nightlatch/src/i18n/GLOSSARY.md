# German translation guide (Night Latch)

Tone: 1951 American noir, dry, laconic first-person narration by private eye Thomas (Tom/Tommy) Rook.
Translate like a good German noir novel translation (Chandler/Hammett style): natural, idiomatic, short sentences.
Keep wit and wordplay — rewrite jokes so they work in German rather than translating literally.

Address: Rook and strangers/officials/clients: **Sie**. Rook & Frank Delaney: **du** (old partners).
Rook & Mabel: Mabel says **du** to Tom ("Tom"), Rook says **du** to Mabel. Rook & Nora: Sie at first (Ch1–6), **du** in the epilogue.
Rook & Hask, Deacon, Sal, Gus, Barnaby: du (old acquaintances / dockside). Kroll/Vey/Vivian/Crane/Kessler/Pettibone/Agnes/police: Sie.

Keep unchanged: personal names (Thomas Rook, Nora Marsh, Walter Marsh, Mabel Finch, Frank Delaney, Dr. Lucian Vey, Vivian Hale,
Evelyn Marsh, Deacon Boone, Sal, Gus Tolliver, Otto Kessler, Harold Abernathy, Wendell Pike, Sister Agnes→Schwester Agnes,
Augustus Crane, Hask, Barnaby, Danny Pruitt, Miss Pruett, Lillian, Cobb, Kerrigan, Kroll, Captain Morrow→Captain Morrow),
places (Brackwater, Hotel Sorrento, The Blue Lantern, Pier 9, Gull Hill, Halcyon House, Marrow Street, St. Brigid's, Bellwether
Apartments, Lyme Street, WBRK), ship names (Meridian, Calypso), company Gullwing Holdings, drug name Lethe, L-7, physostigmine→Physostigmin.
Signs/printed text in the art that the player sees as images stay English; text quoting them may keep the English words.

Fixed terms: notebook=Notizbuch, clue=Hinweis, deduction=Schlussfolgerung, timeline=Zeitleiste, lock picks=Dietriche,
padlock=Vorhängeschloss, gate log=Torbuch, cold store=Kühlhaus, darkroom=Dunkelkammer, safelight=Rotlicht (Dunkelkammerlicht),
steno pad=Stenoblock, transcript/minutes=Protokoll, page one/two/three=Seite eins/zwei/drei, hold (ship)=Laderaum,
hatch=Luke, broadcast=Sendung, ON AIR=ON AIR, retainer=Vorschuss, Lieutenant=Lieutenant, Sergeant (Sgt.)=Sergeant,
Commissioner=Hafenkommissar (Augustus Crane), Harbor Commission=Hafenkommission, Precinct=Revier, evidence box=Beweiskiste.

Short verbs used as hotspot actions (e.g. "Take", "Open", "Search", "Talk to", "Use", "Go to", "Look at"):
translate as German imperative/infinitive UI verbs: Take=Nimm, Open=Öffne, Search=Durchsuche, Use=Benutze, Read=Lies, Push=Drücke,
Pull=Ziehe, Climb=Klettere auf, Consult=Konsultiere... They are shown as "<Verb> <object>" e.g. "Nimm Hut".
Object/hotspot names: short nominative noun phrase without article if the English has none ("hat" -> "Hut", "desk drawer" -> "Schreibtischschublade").

Dialogue options in parentheses like "(Hold something out to him.)" stay in parentheses. Keep length similar; options must stay ≤ 72 characters.
Keep line breaks (\n), quotes style: use German „…“ for quotations inside text where English uses “…” or '…'.
Keep times, numbers, codes (Harbourview 4-170, 417, 226) exactly.
